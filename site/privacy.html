Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Plain-language summary. Meridian Reader is an offline-first iPhone app. Speech runs on your device. There is no Meridian account, no Meridian backend, and no analytics from us. Library items, settings, prepared audio, and downloaded voice models stay on your iPhone. Browse loads websites you open, the same way a browser does. Delete the app to remove the data it stores.

1. Who we are

Meridian Reader is published by Jimmy Yao. This policy describes the iOS app Meridian Reader (bundle ID com.jimmyyao.Reader) and this small public website used for App Store privacy and support links.

2. Scope

This policy covers the Meridian Reader app on iPhone and these GitHub Pages. It does not cover third-party websites you open in Browse, model hosts you download from, or other apps. Those services have their own policies.

3. What the app stores on your iPhone

Meridian Reader keeps app data on your device only, including:

  • Articles, books, folders, favorites, history, and reading progress you save or create
  • Settings such as voice, speed, appearance, and browsing preferences
  • Prepared audio and temporary listening audio files
  • Downloaded on-device voice models (for example Kokoro or Phonon packs)
  • A short App Group inbox used only to hand a shared URL or file from the Share extension into the app on the same device

That data is not uploaded to a Meridian server. We do not operate a Meridian cloud account for the app.

4. What we do not collect

  • No Meridian account, sign-in, or profile
  • No Meridian analytics, crash-reporting service, advertising, or tracking SDK from us
  • No Meridian backend that receives your library, listening history, or voice choice
  • We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context advertising

Apple may collect standard App Store and operating-system diagnostics under Apple’s own policies when you use iOS or the App Store. That is outside Meridian Reader’s control.

5. Browse and the web

Browse uses Apple’s WebKit (WKWebView) to load pages you choose. When you visit a site, that site and its network (and any scripts or trackers the page includes) may receive the same kinds of information a normal browser visit would: your IP address, device or browser details, cookies the site sets, and the page address. Meridian Reader does not insert Meridian analytics into those pages. Optional “Block Ads” uses on-device content-blocking rules to reduce some ads and trackers on pages loaded after you turn it on.

6. Voice model downloads

On-device voices need model files. When you choose to download a voice or engine pack, the app fetches those files from the model host configured for that engine (for example a public model repository) over the network, then stores them in the app’s on-device storage (Application Support). Download happens only when you start it. Speech synthesis itself runs locally on your iPhone after the model is present.

7. Share and Open In

Safari Share and Files Open In can send a URL, PDF, or EPUB into Meridian Reader. The Share extension writes a short payload into a shared App Group container on your iPhone so the main app can open it. That handoff stays on the device. It is not sent to Meridian servers.

8. Microphone and dictation

If you tap the microphone in the Browse address bar, Meridian Reader may use the microphone and speech recognition so you can dictate a search or address. Recognition prefers on-device processing when your language supports it. Dictation is only for that address-bar control. The app does not record listening sessions or upload your library audio for recognition.

9. This website (GitHub Pages)

These privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web server logs (such as IP address and user agent) to operate the host. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site does not set Meridian analytics cookies.

10. Your choices

  • Delete articles, favorites, history, prepared audio, or downloaded voices inside the app where those controls exist.
  • Revoke microphone or speech recognition access in iOS Settings › Meridian Reader.
  • Delete the Meridian Reader app to remove the data in its container and App Group inbox on that device.
  • Questions about this policy: open a GitHub issue on the public repository (see Contact).

11. Children

Meridian Reader is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Meridian service. Because Browse can open the open web, a guardian should decide whether the app is appropriate.

12. Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected here before or when the app behavior ships.

13. Contact

For privacy questions about Meridian Reader, open an issue on the public repository: github.com/haowjy/meridian-reader/issues.
