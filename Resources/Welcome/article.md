# How Meridian Reader Works

Meridian Reader reads web pages, articles and books out loud. The voice runs on your device, so it needs no internet connection and no account, and it keeps reading when you lock the screen or switch to another app.

To start, open the Browser and visit any site. Tap the headphones to hear the page you're on. For a cleaner page, tap the page icon at the start of the address bar, and Meridian shows just the article, without the ads and menus around it.

Tap the bookmark to save a page to your Library. Saved articles stay on your device, so you can read or listen to them later, even with no signal, and each one picks up where you stopped. In the Library, Add a Link saves a page from a link someone sent you, Paste Text saves anything you copied in another app, and Import a File brings in a book, as an EPUB or a PDF. A book becomes a folder of chapters, read one after another.

A voice takes a moment to turn text into speech. Before a flight or a trip underground, choose Prepare Audio on an article or a folder, and Meridian makes the audio ahead of time, so it plays straight away.

To keep up with a blog, or a story told in chapters, add its link with Add a Link and choose Follow. Each time you open the app, new posts and chapters are added to your Library.

To hear a different voice, go to Settings, then Voice. A new voice is used for articles you start from then on. An article you've already listened to keeps its voice; to change it, open the article's menu and choose Voice.

This article stays in your Library. To find it again, choose Help in the menu with three dots, or go to Settings, then About.
